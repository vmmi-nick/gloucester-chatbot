Welcome

This is a simple demonstration page showing how the Gloucester County Constituent Chatbot appears when it is added to a county webpage. A moment after the page loads, a card appears in the corner inviting you to chat. Tap it to open the full chat; if you close it, it folds down to the chat button in the corner, and it stays closed while you move around the site.

Try asking it when the next commissioners meeting is, how to report a pothole on a county road, or use the Report an issue option inside the chat to prepare a report to call in.
